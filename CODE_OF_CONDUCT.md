# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in NUDO to be a good experience for everyone, regardless
of experience level, background, identity, or how new someone is to compilers.
Language design attracts strong opinions; that is fine. Contempt is not.

## Expected behaviour

* **Criticise ideas, not people.** "This design makes X impossible" is useful.
  "This design is stupid" is not, and the useful version is shorter to write.
* **Assume competence and good faith.** Someone who disagrees with you has read
  the same documents and reached a different conclusion.
* **Say what you know and what you do not.** In a pre-alpha project, "I do not
  know" is a complete and useful answer.
* **Be specific.** A report with a reproducible case beats an opinion expressed
  three times. A review that names the rule it is applying beats a preference.
* **Accept that the maintainer decides.** You can argue, and the argument will be
  read; you do not get to overrule the outcome.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* harassment, insults, personal or political attacks;
* discriminatory language or imagery;
* sexual attention or advances of any kind;
* deliberate intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption of a discussion;
* publishing someone's private information without permission;
* dismissing a report of the above rather than addressing it.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, review comments, and any public space where someone represents
the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through GitHub:

* use **Security → Report a vulnerability**
  (<https://github.com/smouj/nudo/security/advisories/new>) — it is private, it
  reaches the maintainer, and it is not a public issue; or
* contact [@smouj](https://github.com/smouj) directly.

Do not open a public issue about someone's behaviour. Reports are handled
confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides, and the response depends on the behaviour and its
history:

1. **A private word** — the behaviour stops, nothing else happens. This is the
   usual outcome and the intended one.
2. **A formal warning**, with the specific behaviour named.
3. **A temporary ban** from project spaces.
4. **A permanent ban.**

The reasoning is recorded privately; the report itself is never published
without the reporter's consent.

## Attribution

This is a small, project-specific code of conduct rather than an adopted
template. Adopting a widely used one — the Contributor Covenant, for example —
would be an improvement, and is welcome as a pull request.
